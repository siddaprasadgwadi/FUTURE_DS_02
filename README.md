# 📊 Social Media Marketing Dashboard

This project presents a comprehensive **Social Media Marketing Dashboard** that visualizes advertising performance across major platforms — Facebook, Google, Instagram, and LinkedIn — over the period from **January to March 2024**.

## 🔍 Overview

The dashboard tracks the effectiveness of marketing campaigns by summarizing:

- 💰 **Total Ad Spend**: ₹308K  
- 🎯 **Average Conversion Rate**: 42.98%  
- ✅ **Total Conversions**: 7,881  
- 📉 **Lead Goal Tracking**: 109 leads vs. 357 goal (-69.47%)  
- 💵 **Total Revenue**: ₹394K  

## 📈 Key Visualizations

- **Revenue vs. Ad Spend Over Time**  
- **Conversion Rate Trends & Conversions by Date**  
- **Revenue Breakdown by Channel** (Pie Chart)  
- **Lead Generation by Month & Channel** (Bar Chart)  
- **Interactive Filters**:
  - Channel-wise breakdown (Facebook, Google, Instagram, LinkedIn)
  - Date range filter (01-01-2024 to 14-03-2024)

## 🧠 Insights Derived

- Instagram Ads generated the **highest revenue** (~27.23%).
- Conversion rates showed **peaks in March 2024**, indicating campaign improvements.
- Lead generation was **most effective in February**, especially via Facebook Ads.
- Despite strong spend and conversion rates, the **lead goal fell short by 69%**, requiring strategic adjustment.
